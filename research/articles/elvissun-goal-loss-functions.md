### Summary

Most people run /goal and long-running agent loops wrong — the 'point it at a task and walk away' model. Top agentic engineers instead give the loop a loss function: an explicit, measurable objective the loop optimizes toward, with a per-iteration progress signal. Without a loss signal an autonomous loop drifts.

### Relevance to dot-agents

Autonomous loops need (a) a defined objective and (b) a way to measure progress each iteration. Relevant to r1-outcome-scoring and the r4 eval harness — make the objective explicit per run, not implicit in 'keep going.'
